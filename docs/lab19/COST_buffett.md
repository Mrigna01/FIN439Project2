# COST — Buffett-inspired memo

**Verdict: own the business.** This judgment concerns business quality rather
than stock price.

I favor earning power that remains positive after capital spending. Costco's
owner earnings rose from $3,501 million to $9,382 million, with ROE above the
15.0% screen threshold in every reported year. That supports the screen's
positive business-quality conclusion.

The path was not uninterrupted: owner earnings declined from $6,745 million to
$6,629 million before recovering. The check compares the first and last years;
it does not establish steady annual improvement. I would keep the business on
the quality shortlist while examining competitive durability and capital
allocation separately. Year-end equity and the lab's simplified owner-earnings
proxy do not prove a moat or an attractive valuation.

For education, not investment advice.
