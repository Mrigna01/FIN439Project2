# NVDA — Buffett-inspired memo

**Verdict: own the business.** This judgment concerns historical business
quality rather than the attractiveness of a share price.

NVIDIA clears the specified ROE and owner-earnings checks. Latest ROE is 76.3%,
and owner earnings rise from $8,132 million to $96,676 million between the first
and last reported years. Those results justify further investigation of the
business's earning power.

I would resist describing this as an uninterrupted record of compounding.
Owner earnings fell to $3,808 million in an intervening year before rising
sharply. Burry's failed cash-conversion check is also relevant: latest operating
cash flow of $102,718 million is below net income of $120,067 million. Positive
owner earnings do not erase that difference. I would investigate the causes
and durability of the reported profitability before committing money; this
accounting screen does not establish competitive permanence or intrinsic value.

For education, not investment advice.
