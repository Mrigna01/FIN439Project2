# COST — Burry-inspired memo

**Verdict: own the business.** This is an accounting-quality judgment rather
than a valuation conclusion.

I want the reported earnings to have cash support. Operating cash flow is at
least net income in every reported year; the latest figures are $15,817 million
and $9,226 million respectively. Latest liabilities of $53,242 million against
equity of $35,803 million produce a ratio of 1.49, below the 2.00 ceiling.

Those observations satisfy this screen. The earlier liabilities/equity ratio
of 2.11 does not fail the specified leverage check because that check applies to
the latest year. I would still investigate working-capital timing and the
composition of obligations before treating strong cash conversion as durable.
Passing these tests neither proves the shares are cheap nor eliminates the
possibility of a future deterioration.

For education, not investment advice.
